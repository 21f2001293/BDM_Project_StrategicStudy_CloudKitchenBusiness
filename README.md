# BDM_Project_StrategicStudy_CloudKitchen

This project explores the Business Development and Management (BDM) aspects of the cloud kitchen business, focusing on the challenges faced by the business owner and their team in this rapidly growing sector. It analyzes key issues such as operational efficiency, customer acquisition, food quality control, delivery logistics, and competitive market dynamics.
The goal of this project is to provide actionable insights and recommendations that can help this cloud kitchen business achieve scalability, profitability, and long-term success in a highly competitive environment.
